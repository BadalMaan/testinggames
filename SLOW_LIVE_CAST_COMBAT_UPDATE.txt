NEW ATTACK CHOREOGRAPHY
The locked round now plays for approximately 8.25 seconds:
1. CHARGE (0–1.05s): energy begins gathering at each fighter's hand.
2. WIND-UP (1.05–2.45s): the action pose fades in gradually; hand energy builds and pulses.
3. RELEASE (2.45–4.25s): selected power artwork leaves the hand and travels toward the centre.
4. COLLISION (4.25–5.15s): both powers compress into a central energy collision.
5. BREAKTHROUGH (5.15–6.10s): the losing power collapses and the winning power surges forward.
6. IMPACT (6.25s): the target receives a hit burst, recoil/knockback, damage number and camera impact.
7. RECOVERY (7.20–8.25s): effects settle before the existing round result and NEXT ROUND control appear.
