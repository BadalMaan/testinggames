POWER VISIBILITY POLISH — 19 NOV 2026

This is a visual readability pass on the existing procedural Canvas power renderer. It does not change turn selection, player names, ATTACK flow, health/damage rules, rounds, hero artwork or arena background.

Changes:
- Repositioned projectile origins slightly toward the visible extended-hand area.
- Increased charge silhouette size and opacity so selected powers are visible before launch.
- Increased travelling projectile scale and kept the two projectiles vertically separated as they approach the clash, avoiding a single merged dot.
- Added wide, layered, moving wake strands for Plasma, Solar, Shadow and generic powers; retained dedicated fire, lightning, water and ice trails.
- Enlarged collision rings and impact shockwave, with radial impact rays and more visible sparks.
- Increased the winning projectile size on its path to the target.
- No power PNG or SVG files are loaded by the combat canvas.

Validation: TypeScript transpilation and CSS parser checks are run before packaging. Full Vite production build and real-browser FPS testing are not available in this environment.
